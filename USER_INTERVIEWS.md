# User Interviews

## Interview 1: Alex Chen
**Role:** CTO at 15-person AI startup
**Company Stage:** Series A

### 3+ Quotes:
1. "I just approve the invoices. I have no idea if we're overpaying."
2. "We use 5 different AI tools. Tracking spend is impossible."
3. "If someone showed me we could save $500/mo, I'd switch today."

### Most Surprising:
Alex spends 30 minutes each month manually calculating AI spend across 5 tools.

### Design Change:
Added "team size" field because Alex has 12 developers using different tool combinations.

## Interview 2: Sarah Jones
**Role:** Freelance Developer
**Company Stage:** Solo

### 3+ Quotes:
1. "I canceled my Cursor subscription because it was too expensive."
2. "Free tier is fine but so limited."
3. "I didn't know I could get discounted credits."

### Most Surprising:
Sarah downgraded to free tools, losing productivity, because she didn't know about discounts.

### Design Change:
Added clear "Credex credits can save you 20%" messaging for solo users.

## Interview 3: Mike Rodriguez

1. "Our VP just asks 'why is AI spend up 30%?' and I have no answer."
2. "We probably need Enterprise but I don't know the minimums."
