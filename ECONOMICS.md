# Unit Economics

## Lead Value Calculation

**Assumptions:**
- Average startup AI spend: $500/month
- Credex discount: 20-30% (avg 25%)
- Credex margin: 10% of transaction

**Per-lead value:**
- 1,000 audits/month
- 10% email capture (100 leads)
- 20% consultation rate (20 consultations)
- 30% purchase rate (6 purchases)
- Average deal: $500/month spend × 12 months × 25% discount = $1,500 annual savings × 10% margin = $150 gross profit per customer

**Customer LTV: $150 × 12 months = $1,800**

## Customer Acquisition Cost

**Channel CAC estimates:**
- Organic/Word-of-mouth: $0
- Hacker News launch: $0
- Slack communities: $0
- Product Hunt: $0
- Referral program: $50 (10% of first purchase)

**Blended CAC: ~$5-10**

## Break-even Math

**Monthly fixed costs:**
- Vercel hosting: $0 (Hobby tier)
- MongoDB Atlas: $0 (Free tier)
- OpenRouter AI: $5 (10,000 requests)
- Resend email: $0 (3,000 emails free)
- **Total: $5/month**

**Breakeven = $5 / $150 gross profit = 0.03 customers/month**

## $1M ARR Path

**$1M ARR = $83,333/month**

**Required at $1,800 LTV:**
- 46 new customers/month
- At 30% purchase rate → 154 consultations/month
- At 20% consultation rate → 770 email captures/month
- At 10% capture rate → 7,700 audits/month

**Daily requirement: 257 audits/day**

**Feasibility:** Yes, with viral sharing (1 share → 3 new audits)

## Key Metrics to Track
- Audit-to-email capture rate (target: 15%)
- Email-to-consultation rate (target: 25%)
- Consultation-to-purchase rate (target: 35%)
