# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Target User
**Engineering Manager at 10-50 person startup** who approves AI tool spend but doesn't track it systematically.

They Google: "How much should we spend on AI tools" or "ChatGPT team plan worth it"

## Distribution Channels

### 1. Hacker News (Launch Day)
- Post "Show HN: I built a free tool that audits your AI spend"
- Target: Tuesday 8am EST for maximum visibility
- Expected: 50-100 users first day

### 2. Indie Hackers Slack
- 20,000+ founders actively discussing tools
- Share audit results of common stacks (Cursor + ChatGPT)

### 3. Reddit
- r/SaaS, r/startups, r/Entrepreneur
- "We audited 50 startups' AI spend. Here's what we found"

### 4. Twitter/X
- Tag AI founders (@levelsio, @shl, @saranormous)
- Share viral audit results with screenshots

## First 100 Users in 30 Days ($0 Budget)

**Week 1:**
- DM 50 founders on X (personalized: "I audited your stack, saved you $X")
- Post on 10 Slack communities

**Week 2:**
- Launch on Product Hunt (free)
- Submit to 5 directories (Uneed, BetaList, etc.)

**Week 3:**
- Write 3 case studies (real audits of public startups)
- Guest post on Indie Hackers

**Week 4:**
- Cold email 50 startups with their audit preview
- Referral program: share = both get Credex discount

## Unfair Distribution Channel
**Credex's existing customer base** of 500+ startups already buying discounted credits. Offer free audit to all customers + affiliate fee for referrals.

## Week 1 Traction Metrics
- 500 audits completed
- 100 email captures
- 10 consultation bookings
- 1-2 credit purchases
